= 6. Keep the human decision explicit

== Learn

The adapter produces evidence; it does not approve its own evidence. A named
reviewer must preserve the source state and limits when deciding whether the
candidate may continue to deeper validation.

== Show

Inspect the bounded decision counters. They count process outcomes only; they
are not VM, model, capacity, latency, cost, or Intel hardware metrics.

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
curl -fsS "$ADAPTER_URL/metrics" | tee "$LAB_DIR/metrics.txt"
grep 'virtualization_ai_301_decisions_total' "$LAB_DIR/metrics.txt"
----

== Do

Create a reviewer-owned decision record. Change `REVIEWER` to your name or lab
identity if desired.

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
export REVIEWER="${REVIEWER:-lab-participant}"
jq -n \
  --arg reviewer "$REVIEWER" \
  --arg decision "ACCEPT_FOR_FURTHER_VALIDATION" \
  --arg evidence_id "$EVIDENCE_ID" \
  --arg source_state "$(jq -r .source_state "$LAB_DIR/allow-response.json")" \
  --arg recorded_at "$(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)" \
  '{
    reviewer:$reviewer,
    decision:$decision,
    evidence_id:$evidence_id,
    source_state:$source_state,
    recorded_at:$recorded_at,
    limits:[
      "not Launchpad certification",
      "not production approval",
      "not performance or capacity proof",
      "not authority to mutate the VM or cluster"
    ]
  }' | tee "$LAB_DIR/human-decision.json" | jq .
----

== Prove

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
jq -e --arg evidence_id "$EVIDENCE_ID" '
  .evidence_id == $evidence_id and
  .reviewer != "" and
  .decision == "ACCEPT_FOR_FURTHER_VALIDATION" and
  (.limits | length) == 4
' "$LAB_DIR/human-decision.json"
----
