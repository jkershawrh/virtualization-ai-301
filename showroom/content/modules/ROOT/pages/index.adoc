= Govern a VM-to-AI modernization decision

An infrastructure team wants to connect an existing virtual machine to an AI
advisor without giving the model authority over the VM or the cluster. Your
job is to decide whether the proposed path is safe enough for a named human to
review.

This 60–75 minute level-301 lab follows one decision from declared intent to a
tamper-evident receipt. You will inspect the real namespace, compare the
declaration with observations, call the governed adapter, force refusal and
abstention, record a human decision, and clean up your learner artifacts.

The outcome `ALLOW_REVIEW` is deliberately narrow: it means the evidence is
complete enough for a human to review. It does *not* authorize a deployment,
migration, promotion, or certification.

== The learning loop

* *Show* — observe the VM, adapter, policy boundary, and current runtime mode.
* *Learn* — understand why identity, network, observability, placement, and
  model checks run in that order.
* *Do* — submit one governed modernization request and three controlled faults.
* *Prove* — retrieve the evidence record, verify inference provenance, record
  human authority, and leave no learner-created residue.

== Truth boundary

The `/healthz` response is authoritative for this session. In `REHEARSAL`, the
adapter uses a deterministic fixture: `source_state == "REHEARSAL"` and
`ai_participated == false`. In `LIVE`, a successful advisory must report
`source_state == "LIVE"` and `ai_participated == true`. Never describe a
rehearsal response as model inference or Intel hardware proof.

Your OpenShift project and the Launchpad session are provisioned for you. Do
not uninstall the lab release; Launchpad owns full environment reclamation.
